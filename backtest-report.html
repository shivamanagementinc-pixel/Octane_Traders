1How the backtest works

No look-ahead bias: every signal is generated only from bars before it, and its outcome is decided only by bars after it.

ComponentDescription
Signal generationSMC scanner detects liquidity sweeps + order blocks + fair value gaps on 15m bars, with a 1H trend bias.
EntryPrice inside an active OB/FVG zone after a liquidity sweep.
Stop-lossBeyond the swept level / zone edge, ATR-scaled, min 5 pips.
Take-profitNext untapped liquidity pool ≥ 20 pips away.
OutcomeForward check of the next 96 bars (24h): which was hit first, TP or SL.
ScoringEach trade's P&L measured in R (risk units). A win = +R:R, a loss = −1R. Expectancy = average R per trade.
ValidationWalk-forward: parameters chosen on the first 30 days (train), then verified on the next 30 days (test).

2The evolution — five measured iterations

Every change below was accepted or rejected based on its effect on out-of-sample expectancy.

Iteration 1 — Raw system REJECTED
9 pairs, all scores ≥70. Result: 5,519 signals, 24.6% win rate, −156R, −0.03R/trade. Verdict: a noise machine — ~92 signals/day, mostly duplicates, break-even-to-losing after costs.
Iteration 2 — Narrow the universe & score band KEPT
Cut to 4 clean pairs (EURUSD, AUDUSD, XAUUSD, USDJPY) and score band 70–89. Result: 411 signals, 35.2% win, +0.41R/trade. The noisy crosses and the 90+ "chase zone" were the drag.
Iteration 3 — Bias alignment KEPT
Trade only WITH the 1H trend (drop counter-trend signals). Result: expectancy +0.41R → +0.62R, win rate 35.7%. Confirmed out-of-sample.
Iteration 4 — Session filter KEPT
Trade only London/NY hours (03:00–17:00 ET). Kills Asian chop and late-NY drift. Final result: 152 signals, 40.3% win, +0.62R/trade.
Iteration 5 — Anti-spam cooldown REFINED
Discovery: a setup that persists for hours re-wins at 50% vs 29% for first-fire signals. So we record everything (feed the report) but limit Telegram pings to 1/pair/hour.

5Two findings that shaped the system

Finding 1 — the "best" signals were the worst
90+ score lost −0.12R/trade

The highest-scoring trades were over-stacked confluences = chasing extended moves. The profitable band is 70–89. We hard-capped the ceiling at 89.

Finding 2 — persistent setups are the best setups
Re-fires win 50% vs 29%

A signal still valid an hour later is far more reliable than a first-fire. So we record every instance but cooldown the alerts — never delete the good trades.
